# Quarterly Business Review

**Period:** Q2 2026
**Prepared for:** Board of Directors
**Classification:** Confidential

## Executive Summary

Revenue grew 23% quarter-on-quarter, driven by enterprise adoption of the document platform. Gross margin improved four points while headcount remained flat. We recommend accelerating the self-serve roadmap to sustain momentum into Q3.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Q1 2026 | Q2 2026 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | $4.2M | $5.2M | +23% |
| Gross margin | 68% | 72% | +4 pts |
| Active teams | 1,850 | 2,410 | +30% |
| Net retention | 108% | 117% | +9 pts |

## Highlights

- Closed the three largest enterprise contracts in company history.
- Shipped the professional standards engine two weeks ahead of plan.
- Support response time fell below one hour for the first time.

## Risks

- Two competitors announced overlapping export features.
- Hiring for the rendering team remains behind plan.

## Recommendations

1. Approve the additional $400K investment in the self-serve funnel.
2. Prioritise the template marketplace for Q3 delivery.
3. Expand the rendering team by three senior engineers.

## Next Steps

The leadership team will present the detailed Q3 operating plan at the July board session.
